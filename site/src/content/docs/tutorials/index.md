Tutorials are different from guides. Guides explain what something does. Tutorials are scenario-driven — you follow a specific person through a specific situation, typing the same prompts they type, seeing roughly what they see.

Each one takes under 10 minutes to complete alongside Claude Code.

---

## Pick your starting point

**New to bettersense?**  
Start with [Set up your stakeholder radar](/tutorials/stakeholder-radar). It's the most foundational skill in the bundle, and it demonstrates how bettersense differs from asking plain Claude.

**Have a promo cycle coming up?**  
Start with [Log a win and turn it into a story](/tutorials/log-a-win). Even if you've been logging, the curation step is where most people are surprised.

**Building an AI feature?**  
Start with [Sanity-check an AI feature before building it](/tutorials/ai-feature-check). It runs through the anti-hype stack — reducer, scientist, eval designer — on a fictional feature pitch.

**Running a team?**  
Start with [The Friday leadership review](/tutorials/friday-review). A 15-minute weekly routine that touches wins, stakeholders, and patterns.
